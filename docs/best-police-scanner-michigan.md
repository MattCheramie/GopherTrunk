# Best Police Scanner for Michigan (2026)

**In Michigan the buying question is simple: can the scanner ride MPSCS?**
The [Michigan Public Safety Communications System](/reference/project-25/) is the
state's [P25](/reference/project-25/) digital trunked network, and the majority of
state, county and municipal agencies operate on it. Before you buy, look up your
county on [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) and note whether your
local MPSCS sites are **P25 Phase II [simulcast](/reference/simulcast/)** or whether
your target agency is still on conventional analog — that picks your scanner.

| Where you are in Michigan | Likely system | Buy this |
|---|---|---|
| Metro Detroit (Wayne, Oakland, Macomb) | MPSCS P25 Phase II, heavy simulcast | [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) / SDS200 |
| Grand Rapids, Lansing, Ann Arbor, Flint | MPSCS P25, some simulcast | [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) or [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) |

> **Metro Detroit is simulcast-heavy.** Where several MPSCS towers transmit the same
> P25 signal at once, overlap zones distort budget digital scanners into silence.
> [Simulcast](/reference/simulcast/) is exactly where a
> [True I/Q SDS](/reference/uniden-sds100/) pulls ahead. Confirm your city's sites on
> RadioReference before you assume you can save money.

## New to scanning

The [Uniden BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) (handheld) programs from your ZIP
code and loads the right MPSCS talkgroups automatically — no frequency tables. Across
the many Michigan counties where sites aren't simulcast it decodes P25 identically to
an SDS and saves you well over $100. In a heavy simulcast metro it can struggle where
the SDS holds a lock, so match the radio to what RadioReference shows for your area.

## Still-analog Michigan

Rural Michigan and much of the Upper Peninsula still run conventional analog — road
commissions, volunteer fire, DNR-adjacent and business users. Where your targets are
analog, you don't need digital: a ~$110
[Uniden BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) covers VHF/UHF, air, marine and rail —
handy near the Great Lakes shipping lanes. See the
[main buyer's guide](/best-police-scanners/) for more.

## The free Michigan alternative: SDR + GopherTrunk

Own a computer? Skip the scanner. A **~$30 [RTL-SDR](/downloads.html) dongle** plus
free, open-source **GopherTrunk** decodes P25 (Phase I and II), DMR, NXDN and TETRA in
software. It records and timestamps **every** MPSCS call, follows unlimited talkgroups
at once, logs radio IDs, and streams to a browser console. The trade-off is a PC and
antenna, and no pocket portability. Read the honest
[scanner vs SDR comparison](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/) and grab the software from
[downloads](/downloads.html).

> **The encryption wall.** No SDS, no BCD436HP, and no SDR decodes
> **[AES encryption](/police-scanner-encryption/)**. Where a Michigan agency encrypts
> on MPSCS, that traffic is gone for everyone. Buy for what's still in the clear.
