# Did We Just Have an Earthquake? How to Check in 30 Seconds

**Real earthquakes post to the USGS map automatically within minutes — so
the fastest answer is [earthquake.usgs.gov](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/):
if nothing appears near you in ten minutes, the shake was something else,
and this page covers both branches.** Here's the check, the impostor list,
and the during-and-after drill worth knowing cold if the answer is yes.

## The 30-second check

1. **[USGS Latest Earthquakes](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/)** — the
   automatic feed; zoom to your area. Magnitude, depth, and epicenter
   appear within a few minutes of any real event.
2. **File "Did You Feel It?"** — one minute, and your report joins the
   real-time felt map that's also how you confirm the neighbors felt it
   too (and how seismologists map intensity — you're contributing data).
3. **Nothing after ten minutes?** It wasn't a quake. Work the impostor
   list on [what was that loud boom](/loud-boom-near-me/) — sonic booms,
   blasting, frost quakes, and transformer faults all produce one-jolt
   "earthquakes," and dispatch radio catches the ones that drew 911 calls.
4. **Texture matters:** a single sharp bang-jolt leans impostor; rolling
   or rattling that builds, sustains seconds, and fades leans earthquake —
   as does a swaying sensation upstairs with hanging lights moving.

## If it was real: the drill, during and after

**During:** **Drop, cover, and hold on** — under a table, arms over head
and neck, hold the leg, ride it out. Running outside is how most US quake
injuries happen (façades and glass come down at the exits), and the doorway
advice died decades ago. In bed: stay, pillow over head.

**After:**

- **Shoes before steps** — broken glass is the classic injury.
- **Nose check:** any gas smell → out, and call from
  [outside](/loud-boom-near-me/). Don't flip switches on the way.
- **Aftershocks are the rule, not the exception** — same drill each time;
  skip elevators for the day.
- **Walk the house like the [post-boom checklist](/loud-boom-near-me/):**
  water heater straps, chimney/masonry cracks, doors that suddenly bind
  (foundation movement tell).
- **Coastal + shaking that was strong or lasted 20+ seconds:** move to
  high ground immediately — the shaking *is* the tsunami warning.

**Before the next one (5 minutes):** enable earthquake alerts on your phone
(ShakeAlert states get seconds of real warning; Android's detection is
built in), strap the water heater, and make sure
[emergency alerts](/emergency-alert-just-went-off/) are on.
