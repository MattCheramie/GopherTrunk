# Best Police Scanner for Illinois (2026)

**Illinois scanning centers on two things: the Chicago metro's dense digital systems and the statewide P25 network (commonly known as STARCOM21) that state and local agencies ride across the whole state.** Chicago, Aurora, Rockford, Joliet and the collar counties run [P25](/reference/project-25/), some of it [simulcast](/reference/simulcast/), while downstate and rural counties still carry conventional analog. Check your county on [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) before buying — that decides which radio below fits.

## The statewide system and the Chicago metro

Illinois' statewide [P25](/reference/project-25/) network — widely known as STARCOM21 — is used by state police and many local agencies, so following it while moving between counties rewards a radio that holds wide-area P25 well. Layer on the Chicago metro's dense systems, some of them [P25 Phase II](/reference/simulcast/) [simulcast](/reference/simulcast/), and you have the classic case for a True I/Q front end. The Uniden [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) and [SDS200](/reference/uniden-sds200/) demodulate simulcast that stalls cheaper scanners, which is why they lead the list for the Chicago area.

> **Collar and downstate counties may not be simulcast.** Where yours isn't, a [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) or [BCD996P2](/reference/uniden-bcd996p2/) gives identical P25 audio for less. Check RadioReference before paying the simulcast premium.

## Beginners: start from your ZIP code

The [Uniden BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) handheld (or BCD536HP base) programs from your Illinois ZIP code and loads the local systems — including the statewide network where it's in range — from its onboard database. No manual frequency tables, full P25 Phase I/II. It's the easiest first digital scanner for Illinois' mix of statewide and metro systems, and it upgrades painlessly as more of your county goes digital.

## Downstate and rural Illinois

Across central and southern Illinois, plenty of fire and public-works traffic is still conventional analog. If RadioReference shows analog for your county, a ~$110 [Uniden BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) covers it plus aircraft, marine, rail and NOAA weather — no need to buy digital for an analog county.

## The free alternative: RTL-SDR + GopherTrunk

With a PC, a **~$30 RTL-SDR dongle running free [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html)** decodes Illinois P25, DMR and NXDN in software, follows unlimited talkgroups at once, and records and timestamps every call to a web console — handy for catching the statewide system and a metro system in parallel. It needs a computer and isn't pocket-portable; compare it in [scanner vs SDR](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/).

## Encryption in Illinois

Some Illinois agencies encrypt part or all of their talkgroups. **No scanner and no SDR — including GopherTrunk — can legally decode encrypted P25.** It is a cryptographic and legal wall. Before buying, confirm your specific agency is still in the clear on [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) and read the [encryption explainer](/police-scanner-encryption/).
