# Best Police Scanner for Georgia (2026)

**In Georgia the split is stark: greater Atlanta is dense digital, and much of the
rest of the state is a patchwork of P25 and lingering analog.**
Many state and local agencies run [Project 25](/reference/project-25/) digital
trunked systems — a statewide shared network plus big county and city systems around
the metro — and in the dense areas those are frequently
[P25 Phase II simulcast](/reference/simulcast/). Look up your county on
[RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) first: whether your agencies are
**simulcast P25** or **plain analog** decides which scanner you need.

| Where you are | Likely system | Buy this |

| Savannah, Augusta, Columbus, Macon | P25, some simulcast | [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) or [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) |

## The easy on-ramp

New to this? The [Uniden BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) programs from your ZIP
code and loads the right talkgroups automatically — no manual frequency entry. Across
much of Georgia outside the Atlanta simulcast core it decodes P25 perfectly and saves
you well over $100 versus an SDS. In a heavy simulcast metro it can stumble where the
SDS won't, so match it to what RadioReference shows for your county.

## Still-analog Georgia

Rural south and middle Georgia keep a lot of conventional analog alive — volunteer
fire, EMS, public works, and business itinerant users. If your targets are analog,
you don't need a digital radio at all: a ~$110
[Uniden BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) covers the VHF/UHF, air, marine and rail
bands. See the [main buyer's guide](/best-police-scanners/) for more analog picks.

## The free Georgia alternative: SDR + GopherTrunk

Own a computer? Skip the hardware. A **~$30 [RTL-SDR](/downloads.html) dongle** plus
free, open-source **GopherTrunk** decodes P25 (Phase I and II), DMR, NXDN and TETRA in
software. It records and timestamps **every** call, follows unlimited talkgroups at
once, and streams to a browser console. The catch is that it needs a PC and a decent
antenna and won't fit in a pocket. Read the honest
[scanner vs SDR breakdown](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/) and pull the software from
[downloads](/downloads.html).

> **The encryption wall.** No SDS, no BCD436HP, and no SDR decodes
> **[AES encryption](/police-scanner-encryption/)**. Where a Georgia agency encrypts,
> it's silent for everyone. Buy for what's still in the clear.
