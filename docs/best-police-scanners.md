# Best Police Scanners (2026 Buyer's Guide)

**The best police scanner is the cheapest one that can hear your agencies.**
Before you spend a dollar, look up your county on
[RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) and note whether police, fire,
and EMS are **analog or digital**, **conventional or [trunked](/reference/trunked-radio/)**,
and whether they're **[simulcast](/reference/simulcast/)** or **encrypted**. Those
four facts decide everything below.

## How to choose

- **Digital + simulcast metro?** [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) /
  [SDS200](/reference/uniden-sds200/). Nothing else decodes simulcast as well.
- **Digital, no simulcast, want it easy?** [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/)
  (handheld) or [BCD536HP](/reference/uniden-bcd536hp/) (base) — ZIP-code start.
- **Digital, no simulcast, save money?** [BCD996P2](/reference/uniden-bcd996p2/) /
  [BCD325P2](/reference/uniden-bcd325p2/).
- **Want DMR + NXDN with no upgrade fee?** [Whistler TRX-1](/reference/whistler-trx-1/) /
  [TRX-2](/reference/whistler-trx-2/).
- **Still analog?** [BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) or
  [SR30C](/reference/uniden-sr30c/). See [cheap police scanners](/cheap-police-scanner/).
- **Have a PC and want everything recorded?** Skip the radio: a
  [$30 RTL-SDR](/reference/rtl-sdr/) + free [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html). Read
  [scanner vs GopherTrunk](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/) first.

## Bottom line

The **Uniden SDS100/SDS200** is the best police scanner for most people because
simulcast is where cheaper radios fail — but if your area isn't simulcast, a
**BCD436HP** or **BCD996P2** saves $150+ for identical audio. Whatever you buy,
**no scanner decodes [encryption](/police-scanner-encryption/)**, and if you own a
computer, trying a **[$30 dongle with GopherTrunk](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/)** first
costs almost nothing and records every call.
