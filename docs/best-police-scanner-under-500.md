# Best Police Scanner Under $500 (2026)

**This is the first budget where a real digital police scanner fits — no
compromises, no dongles required.** Under $200 you were choosing between analog
handhelds and a $30 SDR. At $500, dedicated **P25 Phase I/II** radios like the
[BCD325P2](/reference/uniden-bcd325p2/) and [BCD996P2](/reference/uniden-bcd996p2/)
comfortably fit, and the ZIP-code-friendly
[BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) sits right at the edge.

## What changes at $500

Digital P25 is where cheaper radios hit a wall — and $500 clears it. Every pick
below decodes **P25 Phase I and Phase II**, the modes most metro police, fire, and
EMS now use. The only thing this budget *doesn't* fully buy is **best-in-class
simulcast** performance; that's the [SDS](/best-police-scanners/) territory
(~$650), noted below.

> **Check for simulcast.** On [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/),
> see if your P25 system is **simulcast**. These sub-$500 radios decode P25 Phase
> II but only *fairly* on tough simulcast — the SDS100/SDS200 True I/Q front ends
> are markedly better there and are worth the stretch if your metro is simulcast.

## How to choose within the budget

- **Fixed at home or in the car, best value?** [BCD996P2](/reference/uniden-bcd996p2/).
- **Want to carry it, most money left over?** [BCD325P2](/reference/uniden-bcd325p2/).
- **Hate manual programming?** [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) — enter your
  ZIP and go (borderline on price).
- **Metro runs simulcast?** Save for an [SDS100/SDS200](/best-police-scanners/)
  instead; the sub-$500 radios only decode it *fairly*.

## The free companion

Even after buying a digital scanner, a **[$30 RTL-SDR](/reference/rtl-sdr/)** with
free [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html) is worth adding: it decodes P25/DMR/NXDN in
software and, unlike a handheld, **records and timestamps every call** to a
searchable log with a web console. It needs a PC, so it complements rather than
replaces a scanner — see [scanner vs SDR](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/).

## Still no decoding encryption

As always: **no scanner under $500, and none at any price, decodes AES-encrypted
police.** These radios hear unencrypted P25, DMR, and NXDN only. If your agency
[encrypted dispatch](/police-scanner-encryption/), spend on fire, EMS, and open
neighboring agencies instead — the radio isn't the limitation, the cipher is.
