# Best Police Scanner for Pennsylvania (2026)

**Pennsylvania scanning is shaped by one thing more than any other: the statewide P25 network that state agencies and many counties ride, layered under the big-city systems in Philadelphia and Pittsburgh.** Both metros run digital [P25](/reference/project-25/), some countywide systems use [simulcast](/reference/simulcast/), and rural counties still carry conventional analog. Which talkgroups you can actually hear depends on where you are, so check your county on [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) before buying.

## The statewide system and why True I/Q helps

Pennsylvania's statewide [P25](/reference/project-25/) network spans the whole commonwealth, and following it as you travel between counties is exactly the kind of wide-area, sometimes-[simulcast](/reference/simulcast/) tracking that a True I/Q radio does best. The Uniden [SDS100](/reference/uniden-sds100/) and [SDS200](/reference/uniden-sds200/) hold onto weaker P25 signals and demodulate simulcast that leaves cheaper radios stuttering — the reason they top the list for anyone who wants both the statewide traffic and their local metro system.

> **Not every county is simulcast.** Where yours isn't, a [BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) or [BCD996P2](/reference/uniden-bcd996p2/) decodes the same P25 audio for less. Verify on RadioReference before paying the simulcast premium.

## Beginners: let the ZIP code do the work

The [Uniden BCD436HP](/reference/uniden-bcd436hp/) handheld (or BCD536HP base) programs from your ZIP code and loads the local systems — including the statewide network in your area — from its onboard database. No manual frequency entry. It is the easiest way to start scanning Pennsylvania's mix of statewide and county systems.

## Rural Pennsylvania: analog still matters

Across the northern tier and the rural center of the state, plenty of fire and public-works traffic remains conventional analog. If RadioReference shows analog for your county, a ~$110 [Uniden BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) covers it plus aircraft, marine, rail and NOAA weather — no reason to buy a digital radio for an analog area.

## The free alternative: RTL-SDR + GopherTrunk

Own a PC? A **~$30 RTL-SDR dongle running free [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html)** decodes Pennsylvania P25, DMR and NXDN in software, tracks unlimited talkgroups at once, and records and timestamps every call to a web console. It needs a computer and isn't pocket-portable, so weigh it against a handheld in [scanner vs SDR](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/).

## Encryption in Pennsylvania

Some Pennsylvania agencies encrypt part or all of their talkgroups. **No scanner and no SDR — including GopherTrunk — can legally decode encrypted P25.** It is a cryptographic and legal barrier. Before buying, confirm your specific agency is still unencrypted on [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/) and read the [encryption explainer](/police-scanner-encryption/).
