pocket.** What it does *not* buy is a dedicated digital scanner: the cheapest of

polished analog radio and the best-value digital route there is.

## What $200 actually buys

- **A great analog handheld** — the [BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/) or
  [SR30C](/reference/uniden-sr30c/), with money to spare. Perfect if your police,
  fire, and EMS are still analog.
- **A digital-capable SDR rig** — a [$30 RTL-SDR](/reference/rtl-sdr/) plus free
  [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html), which decodes **P25 digital** that no analog
  handheld can. Uses a PC you already own.
- **What it does *not* buy:** a standalone **digital** scanner. Those start at
  ~$380 with the [BCD325P2](/best-police-scanner-under-500/) — see
  [under $500](/best-police-scanner-under-500/).

> **Check analog vs digital first.** Look up your county on
> [RadioReference](https://www.radioreference.com/). Analog agencies → a handheld
> is perfect. Digital P25 → the SDR is the only in-budget way to hear it.

## The standout value pick

The most useful thing you can do with a $200 budget is *not* spend most of it. A
[$30 RTL-SDR](/reference/rtl-sdr/) with free [GopherTrunk](/downloads.html)
decodes the **digital** systems a $150 analog handheld physically cannot, records
and timestamps every call, and streams to a web console — all on a computer you
already own. The honest catch: it needs that PC and a little setup, so it isn't
pocketable like a handheld. Weigh it in our
[scanner vs SDR comparison](/police-scanner-vs-sdr/).

## Still can't beat encryption

At $200, $30, or $2,000, the answer is the same: **no scanner and no SDR decodes
AES-encrypted police.** It's a [hard cryptographic and legal wall](/police-scanner-encryption/).
If your department encrypted, put the budget toward fire, EMS, and neighboring
agencies still in the clear.

## Bottom line

Under $200, the smart money is a **[$30 RTL-SDR](/reference/rtl-sdr/) + free
[GopherTrunk](/downloads.html)** — it's the only in-budget path to **digital**
police and it barely dents your wallet. If your agencies are analog (or you want a
true grab-and-go radio), the **[BC125AT](/reference/uniden-bc125at/)** or
**[SR30C](/reference/uniden-sr30c/)** are excellent handhelds. A *dedicated*
digital scanner means stepping up to [under $500](/best-police-scanner-under-500/)
territory — and nothing, at any price, decodes
[encryption](/police-scanner-encryption/).
