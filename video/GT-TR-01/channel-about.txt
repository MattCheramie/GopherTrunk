GopherTrunk — trunked radio, decoded.

GopherTrunk is an open-source SDR trunking scanner and decoder for P25, DMR, NXDN, and TETRA. This channel is the video edition of the GopherTrunk Field Guide: plain-language courses that explain how trunked radio actually works, one idea at a time — the channel pool, the control channel, talkgroups, channel grants, FDMA and TDMA, and everything they build into.

Every video has a full written article with diagrams and sources in the Field Guide:
https://gophertrunk.org/reference/

The software is free and open source:
https://github.com/MattCheramie/GopherTrunk

New course pillars monthly. Shorts during the week, deep dives on weekends.
