That's the trunked radio pillar. Five ideas, and they interlock: a pool of shared channels, a control channel coordinating it, talkgroups giving users a stable identity, grants moving each call to its channel — and FDMA or TDMA deciding how those channels share the spectrum.

Everything else in trunking — multisite networks, roaming, encryption, the protocol wars between P25, DMR, and TETRA — builds on these five. When you're ready, those are waiting in the Field Guide.
