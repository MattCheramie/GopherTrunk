# GT-TR-01.00 — Cold open

Somewhere in your city, right now, a dispatcher is talking to a hundred radios — on a frequency that didn't exist a second ago and will be gone a second after they stop.

Police, fire, transit, utilities — hundreds of groups, sharing a handful of channels, coordinated by one data stream that never stops and never speaks.

Decode that one stream, and the entire system opens up: every call announces itself before it starts, and a single software-defined radio can follow all of it at once.

This is how trunked radio works — the pool, the coordinator, the virtual channels, the grants, and the two ways calls share the air. Five ideas, and by the end, you can read a trunked system like a map.
