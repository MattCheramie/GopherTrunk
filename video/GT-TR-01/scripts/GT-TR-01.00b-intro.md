**[V: introcard welcome]**
Welcome to the GopherTrunk Field Guide — the video edition. This course is the trunked radio pillar: the concepts that let many users share a small pool of channels, explained one idea at a time.

**[V: agenda list]**
Here is the route. We start with trunked radio itself — the architecture, and why statistics make it work. Then the control channel, the data stream that coordinates everything. Then talkgroups, the virtual channels you actually listen to. Then channel grants, the messages that start every call. And finally {FDMA|F D M A} and {TDMA|T D M A}, the two ways calls share the spectrum.

**[V: introcard standalone]**
Every segment stands alone — each one starts from its own definition, so you can jump straight to the idea you need. And every segment has a full written article in the Field Guide at gophertrunk dot org, linked below, with diagrams, variants, and sources.

**[V: introcard start]**
That's the map. Let's start where the whole idea starts: with the problem of too many groups and too few frequencies.
