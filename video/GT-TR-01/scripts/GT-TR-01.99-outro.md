# GT-TR-01.99 — Outro
slug: _outro

**[V: outrosum recap]**
That's the trunked radio pillar. Five ideas, and they interlock: a pool of shared channels, a control channel coordinating it, talkgroups giving users a stable identity, grants moving each call to its channel — and {FDMA|F D M A} or {TDMA|T D M A} deciding how those channels share the spectrum.

**[V: outrosum apply]**
Everything else in trunking — multisite networks, roaming, encryption, the protocol wars between {P25|P twenty-five}, {DMR|D M R}, and {TETRA|tetra} — builds on these five. When you're ready, those are waiting in the Field Guide.
