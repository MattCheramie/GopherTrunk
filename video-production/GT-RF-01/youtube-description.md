## Description

When you turn a dial and a voice comes out — what actually happened? This
course takes you from "what is a radio wave" to reading a spectrum display,
counting in decibels, and following a complete link budget, in 22
self-contained chapters. Every chapter has a full written article in the
GopherTrunk Field Guide (links below).
